# Viva Questions — P4 — Verification, Scoring & Research Engineer

Target ≈13 of the team's 50. Write the answers **in your own words** after you have built the
thing. An answer copied from an AI is worth zero marks in the room.

Rehearsal rule: another team member quizzes you on these, and you get quizzed on *their*
module too. Gaps found there are the real audit.

1. What exactly is an LLM hallucination, and how is it different from misinformation?
2. Why is semantic similarity insufficient for fact verification? Give a concrete failure.
3. What does NLI actually compute, and why does premise/hypothesis order matter?
4. Why combine NLI with a knowledge graph? What does each catch that the other misses?
5. Derive the aggregation formula and justify each factor.
6. How exactly is the confidence score calculated, and is it a probability? Defend your answer.
7. What is calibration, what is ECE, and what were your numbers before and after?
8. How do you handle conflicting sources?
9. What happens when no evidence is retrieved — and why is that the most important design decision in the project?
10. How do you prevent false positives, and what is your measured FPR on true-but-unretrievable claims?
11. What is the actual research contribution, and what experiment would falsify it?
12. Which ablation mattered most, and what does that tell you?
13. What are the computational costs, and what does the adaptive policy buy you?
14. What are the limitations of this architecture?
